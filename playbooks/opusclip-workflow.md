# OpusClip workflow (about 10 minutes per clip)

1. **Find the moment.** Use the streamer's most-viewed clips from the last 24 hours, not full VODs. OpusClip Pro includes 300 minutes a month, so 1-minute inputs go much further than a 12-hour stream.
2. **Cut it.** Paste the clip link (or download and upload the file). 9:16 vertical, captions on, 15 to 60 seconds.
3. **Hook and title.** The reaction should be in the first 2 seconds. Title pattern: "<Streamer> <reaction> at GTA 6's <moment>".
4. **Tag for the campaign.** Required hashtags, mentions and watermark rules, plus #GTA6 and the streamer's name.
5. **Post and submit.** Post to all four accounts, then submit each post's link to its campaign.

## Clip types that work at launch, in order

1. First reactions: opening scene, first car stolen, first look at the map
2. Chaos: police chases, crashes, rampages that go wrong
3. Bugs and glitches
4. Streamers reacting to each other, or chat roasting them
