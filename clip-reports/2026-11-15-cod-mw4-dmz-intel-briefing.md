## What the edit needs to do

1. **Cut the dead space.** Trim each voice gap down to its single best action shot, as in the running order below.
2. **More action.** Keep the machine-gun shot, the muzzle flash, the laser grid and the firefight. Drop black frames, the fog aerial tail and slow pans first.
3. **A stronger story.** Open cold on the laser grid and "Rogue operators were spotted gaining entry", then tell the briefing in four beats with a small label for each: **THE INTEL → THE THREAT → THE OBJECTIVE → YOUR CALL.**

Target length: **about 50 seconds** (down from 64). The campaign minimum is 10 seconds.
