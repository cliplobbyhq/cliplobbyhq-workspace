# Clip brief: N3on – R3born Episode 2 (HardScope)

## Source video

- **Video:** R3born Episode 2, HardScope YouTube channel (HQ footage from the ClipFarm campaign), about 17 minutes
- **Streamer:** N3on, a Kick IRL streamer, with a guest, Sarah
- **Target market:** young, mostly male Gen Z viewers in the US, UK, Canada and Australia who follow streamer culture
- **Vibe of this video:** a calorie-burning bet across three "women's workouts" (Pilates, pole dancing, aerial silks). N3on loses every round, tries to cheat, and gets waxed at the end. Loud, roast-heavy, physical comedy.

## What a good clip from this video looks like

- **Keep:** N3on struggling, shaking or failing at moves; Sarah and the instructors roasting him; calorie reveals; the cheating moment; the wax punishment; any moment he swears or protests in disbelief
- **Avoid:** instructions with nobody reacting, walking between studios, the song with explicit lyrics during Pilates, any strip-club or stripper framing, gender jabs ("genes of a woman"), anything mentioning FaZe or politics
- **Tone:** funny and chaotic, laughing with N3on rather than humiliating him. Never make HardScope look bad.

## Output settings for every clip

- Vertical 9:16, keep faces large, cropped tight on N3on and whoever is reacting
- 15 to 45 seconds (never under 7), starting on the wildest second with no setup
- Big, bold, word-by-word captions in the centre, readable with the sound off. Star out f-words in the captions.
- No added music over speech
- End on the reaction, just before the moment fully resolves, so viewers want the full episode
- Episode 2 footage only, with audio always from the same scene
